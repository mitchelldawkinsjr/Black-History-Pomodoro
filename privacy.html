Black History Pomodoro Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Black History Pomodoro is a browser extension that runs a Pomodoro timer and reveals sourced Black history facts with rights-checked archival imagery during rest breaks. This policy explains what information the extension handles.

Summary

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not run advertising or analytics in the extension.
  • We do not require an account or collect your name or email through the extension.
  • Timer state, tasks, and discovery progress are stored locally in your browser.

Information stored on your device

The extension uses Chrome’s local storage (chrome.storage) to save:

  • Pomodoro timer state (focus/break phase, remaining time, pause state)
  • Your daily task list
  • Progress on the current discovery (break count, canvas snapshot, unlocked clues)
  • IDs of discoveries already shown, to avoid repeats
  • Extension settings such as focus and break durations

This data stays on your device unless you uninstall the extension or clear extension storage. We do not receive copies of this data on our servers from the extension storage.

Network requests

The extension may connect to:

  • The Black History API (blackhistory.360web.cloud) — to fetch sourced historical discoveries and approved media
  • Image hosts referenced by those discoveries (for example Wikimedia Commons or other rights-checked archival CDNs) — to load imagery for the break overlay

Discovery requests go to The Black History API. We do not sell that traffic or use it for advertising. Image requests follow the license and attribution of each matched source, and that credit is shown in the break overlay.

Permissions

Black History Pomodoro requests the following browser permissions:

  • storage — save timer, tasks, and discovery progress locally
  • alarms — run the timer when the popup is closed
  • tabs — find an active browser tab to show the break overlay
  • scripting — inject the overlay when a break starts
  • host access (<all_urls>) — display the fullscreen break overlay on whichever website you are viewing during a rest break

The extension does not read page content for tracking or advertising. Host access is used only to show and hide the break overlay.

Children’s privacy

Black History Pomodoro is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the extension changes. The “Last updated” date at the top will reflect the latest version.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Open an issue on GitHub or contact the developer through the Chrome Web Store listing.
