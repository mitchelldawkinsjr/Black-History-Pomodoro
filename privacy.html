Paint Pomodoro Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 18, 2026

Paint Pomodoro is a browser extension that runs a Pomodoro timer and displays watercolor overlays of public-domain artworks from The Metropolitan Museum of Art during rest breaks. This policy explains what information the extension handles.

Summary

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not run advertising or analytics in the extension.
  • We do not require an account or collect your name or email through the extension.
  • Timer state, tasks, and painting progress are stored locally in your browser.

Information stored on your device

The extension uses Chrome’s local storage (chrome.storage) to save:

  • Pomodoro timer state (focus/break phase, remaining time, pause state)
  • Your daily task list
  • Progress on the current painting (break count, canvas snapshot, unlocked clues)
  • IDs of artworks already shown, to avoid repeats
  • Extension settings such as focus and break durations

This data stays on your device unless you uninstall the extension or clear extension storage. We do not receive copies of this data on our servers.

Network requests

The extension may connect to:

  • The Metropolitan Museum of Art API (collectionapi.metmuseum.org) — to fetch public-domain artwork metadata
  • The Met image CDN (images.metmuseum.org) — to load artwork images for the watercolor reveal

These requests are made directly from your browser to The Met. We do not proxy or log that traffic. Please refer to The Met’s Open Access policy for their terms.

Permissions

Paint Pomodoro requests the following browser permissions:

  • storage — save timer, tasks, and painting progress locally
  • alarms — run the timer when the popup is closed
  • tabs — find an active browser tab to show the break overlay
  • scripting — inject the overlay when a break starts
  • host access (<all_urls>) — display the fullscreen break overlay on whichever website you are viewing during a rest break

The extension does not read page content for tracking or advertising. Host access is used only to show and hide the break overlay.

Children’s privacy

Paint Pomodoro is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the extension changes. The “Last updated” date at the top will reflect the latest version.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Open an issue on GitHub or contact the developer through the Chrome Web Store listing.
